# Stylish Naari — Website Brand Guidelines

**Visual direction and design system for the Shopify storefront**
Version 1.0 · September 2026 · Source of truth for all homepage, template and Shopify implementation decisions

---

## How to use this document

This document governs every visual and verbal decision on the website. If a design choice is not covered here, resolve it with the principle that runs through all of it:

> **Quiet UI. Expressive textiles.**
> The sarees bring the colour. The interface brings the structure.

When two rules seem to conflict, use this order (from the brief's reference hierarchy):

1. The campaign photography
2. The real colours, patterns and craft of the sarees
3. Contemporary Indian fashion sensibility
4. Minimal editorial ecommerce principles
5. Restraint, the way contemporary Indian lifestyle brands practise it (a benchmark for how far to hold back, not a model to copy)

---

## 1. Brand Essence

**Banarasi, in the present tense.**

Stylish Naari makes Banarasi sarees for women who wear them now: to a dinner, a friend's sangeet, an opening, a Tuesday that deserves it. The weaving is centuries old. How it is worn and presented is current.

Three words sum up the brand:

| **Woven** | **Coloured** | **Current** |
|---|---|---|
| Real Banarasi craft: silk, zari, motifs made on a loom. The craft gives the brand its authority. | Jewel tones and bold contrast borders. Colour is the brand's signature, and it comes from the cloth. | Styled, shot and presented as fashion, not as heritage costume. |

**What the brand is not:** a bridal house, a heritage museum, a palace fantasy, or a discount marketplace.

## 2. Positioning

**Positioning statement**
A contemporary Indian saree brand rooted in Banarasi weaving, designed for modern Indian women who want craft that feels like fashion.

**Where we sit**

| We are | Rather than |
|---|---|
| Contemporary | Traditional |
| Premium | Luxurious |
| Sophisticated | Ornate |
| Expressive | Flashy |
| Culturally rooted | Nostalgic |
| Fashion-forward | Bridal |
| Warm | Sterile |
| Confident | Loud |

**Where the premium feel comes from**
The premium feel comes from *how things are presented*, not from signals of expense. It is built from five things:

1. Photography that looks like a campaign, not a catalogue
2. Scale: images big enough to see the zari
3. Space: generous margins and unhurried sections
4. Typography: a disciplined grotesk, set well
5. Editing: fewer products, fewer words, fewer UI elements

It is **never** built from gold gradients, ornamental frames, royal imagery or luxury copywriting.

---

## 3. Audience

**Primary customer**
Indian women, broadly 26–45, urban or urban-connected, fashion-literate. She follows Indian designers as closely as global ones. She owns her mother's sarees and wants her own, ones that look like *her* choice rather than an inheritance.

**What she values**
Colour · craft she can see and feel · good design · quality she can trust · presentation that doesn't talk down to her

**When she wears a saree**
Festive occasions · weddings (as a guest, not a bride) · cultural events · dinners · celebrations · increasingly, as everyday dressing

**What she is tired of**
Websites that look like wedding invitations. Copy about "timeless elegance". Discount pop-ups. Product grids where every saree looks the same because they're all shot flat on the same beige.

**The job of the website**
Make Banarasi feel like something she'd wear *this season*, not something she keeps for an occasion.

## 4. Visual Personality

| Quality | Level | What it means in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Modern | ●●●●● High | Grotesk typography, clean grid, contemporary interaction patterns |
| Contemporary | ●●●●● High | Styling, copy and composition belong to now |
| Minimal | ●●●●● High | Fewest possible UI elements; no decoration |
| Premium | ●●●●● High | Through restraint, scale, space and craft, never through ornament |
| Editorial | ●●●●○ Med–High | Composed layouts, varied image scale, magazine rhythm |
| Colourful | ●●●●● High — *through imagery only* | The sarees carry the colour; the UI does not |
| Traditional | ●●●○○ Medium | Present in the product and craft story, not in the UI |
| Heritage | ●●●○○ Medium | One dedicated section (The Art of Banarasi); not everywhere |
| Luxury | ●●○○○ Low–Med | A price point, not an aesthetic |
| Playful | ●●○○○ Low–Med | In the styling and the occasional line of copy, never in the UI |
| Ornate | ●○○○○ Low | The weave is ornate so the website doesn't have to be |
| Decorative UI | ●○○○○ Low | No motifs, frames, dividers or pattern fills |

**Rule of thumb:** if you remove the photography and the site feels empty, the UI is right. If it still feels "Indian" without the photography, the UI is doing too much.

## 5. Art Direction

### 5.1 What the existing campaign establishes

The five campaign photographs define the visual world. Their shared grammar:

| Element | What the photography does | What the website takes from it |
|---|---|---|
| **Light** | Single hard key light, raking shadows, spotlight pools on the floor (image 02), window-light diagonals (image 03) | Drama belongs to the image. The UI stays flat and shadowless. |
| **Backdrop** | Warm clay, terracotta-sand and plaster walls; seamless studio floors | UI neutrals are warm, pulled from these walls, never cold grey or pure white |
| **Graphic device** | A single large red disc behind the model (images 04, 05) | One bold shape against a quiet field. The website copies the *principle* (one strong element, lots of quiet) and never the shape itself. |
| **Props** | Few and specific: a mid-century wooden chair, plaster steps, a painted elephant stool, a block-printed rug | Cultural references appear in the image, selectively. The UI adds none of its own. |
| **Pose** | Seated, turned away, hand to cheek, head in hands; relaxed, introspective, in the moment | Copy tone mirrors this: at ease, a little knowing, never performing |
| **Negative space** | 30–50% of every frame is empty wall | Give images room. Never fill the image's own negative space with UI unless the composition calls for it. |
| **Colour** | The saree is always the most saturated object in the frame | The saree must also be the most saturated object on the page |

The current five images carry the launch, but the system needs more to scale:

4. **Flat-lay or draped-form shots** for every SKU, for collection pages and PDPs *only* (never the homepage's main visual language).
5. **At least one lighter, everyday styling story**, to support an Everyday Edit later.

## 6. Photography Direction

For all future shoots and for selecting and cropping existing images.

### 6.1 Shoot principles

- **Model + saree are the subject.** Nothing else in the frame competes for attention.
- **One idea per set.** One backdrop colour, one prop, one graphic element at most.
- **Hard, directional light.** Keep the clean shadow pools and diagonals of the current campaign. Avoid flat, even catalogue lighting.
- **Warm architectural backdrops:** clay, plaster, terracotta, sandstone, limewash. An occasional bold colour field (like the red disc) works if it is a single flat shape.
- **Pose with intent:** seated, turned, mid-gesture, looking away. Avoid the square-on catalogue stance.
- **Styling:** contemporary blouses (sleeveless, halter, mirror-work), sculptural gold cuffs, one flower element. Keep jewellery to 1–2 statements.
- **Show the drape.** At least one frame per saree must show the full fall of the pallu and the border.

### 6.2 Avoid

Palace interiors and jharokhas · wedding mandaps and venue shoots · temple architecture · flower walls and heavy floral set-dressing · stacks of bridal jewellery · catalogue poses with hands on hips · outdoor golden-hour "pre-wedding" aesthetics · stock photography · any set where the backdrop is more ornate than the saree.

*Note on image E:* the painted forest mural and oversized lotuses sit at the edge of what the brand allows. It works as a single image, cropped tight. Don't make it the direction for future shoots.

### 6.3 Crop system

Four crop types. Every image placement on the site uses one of them.

| Crop | Ratio | Frames | Must preserve | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Full-body vertical** | 3:4 (or native 2:3) | Head to hem, with floor | Face, full drape, hem border, silhouette | New Arrivals, Festive, PDP main |
| **Medium portrait** | 4:5 | Crown to mid-thigh | Face and expression, blouse, pallu over shoulder, one hand | Shop by Edit (large), PDP secondary |
| **Textile detail** | 1:1 or 5:4 | A section of drape, border meeting body, pallu fall | The border, zari, one complete motif | Shop by Edit (small), card hover |
| **Wide campaign** | 16:9 desktop / 21:9 ultra-wide | Full scene with extended backdrop | Model, the scene's graphic device (disc, light pool), breathing room | Hero, campaign banners |

A fifth, **textile macro**, is extreme close-up (1:1 or 3:5), used only in Colour Story and Art of Banarasi.

**Crop rules**
- Never crop through the face, hands or the hem border.
- Never crop so that a motif is cut in half at the frame edge. Move the frame until one complete buti or border repeat is visible.
- Set a Shopify **focal point** on every image (Admin → Files → image → focal point) so responsive crops hold the subject.
- Recommended focal points for the current set: A — 48% / 55% · B — 62% / 50% · C — 50% / 55% · D — 58% / 55% · E — 52% / 50%.

### 6.4 Photography ↔ UI relationship

| When the image… | …the UI |
|---|---|
| Is visually rich | Gets quieter: fewer elements, smaller type |
| Has intense colour | Sits on neutral ivory, never on another colour |
| Has dramatic lighting | Keeps typography light-weight and small |
| Shows an intricate weave | Adds no pattern, texture or motif anywhere near it |
| Has strong composition | Gets no overlays, badges, frames or graphic devices |

## 7. Hero Direction

### 7.1 The image

**Image A (`saree image 05.png`)**: magenta saree, back view, head in hands, large red disc behind.

Why it's the hero: it's the most fashion-led frame in the set. The model's anonymity makes it about the saree and the mood rather than a face. The red disc gives the page one graphic anchor, and the magenta and marigold of the saree are the most saturated thing on screen. It is unmistakably Indian (gajra, drape, disc as sun or bindi) without a single cliché.

### 7.2 Composition

- **Full-bleed**, edge to edge, `100svh` minus announcement bar on desktop.
- The **disc sits at the top of the frame** with the model's head just below its lower edge. The disc works as a halo, so **never** place text over it.
- The **headline block sits bottom-left**, aligned to grid column 1, its baseline level with the chair legs, in the floor-shadow zone where contrast is naturally highest. This is chosen for balance, not because it's "the empty side": the model sits centre-left and the pallu trails right, so bottom-left text completes a diagonal from disc → model → pallu → headline.
- **Header floats transparently** over the top edge in ivory.
- Nothing else on the image: no carousel dots, no scroll prompt, no secondary CTA.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Shop  Edits  Craft  About     STYLISH NAARI     Search Bag(0)│
│                                                              │
│                        ██████████████                        │
│                     ████████████████████                     │
│                     ████████████████████                     │
│                        ██████████████                        │
│                            ◖ model ◗                          │
│                           ▟███████▙                          │
│                           ▐█ chair █▙▄▄▄▄ pallu              │
│                                                              │
│  TRADITION,                                                  │

│  Explore the collection →                                    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 7.3 Desktop landscape problem, and the fix

Image A is a 2:3 portrait. A 16:9 frame cropped from it would cut the model at the waist.

- **Launch requirement:** extend the backdrop to 16:9 (1920×1080 minimum, ideally 2880×1620) and 21:9. The wall and floor are plain seamless paper with a soft gradient, which makes this a low-risk retouch. Keep the model at the centre-left third.
- **Fallback, if the extension isn't ready:** show the portrait image at full height, centred, on a full-bleed field of the sampled backdrop colour (`--color-studio-clay`), with a 200px soft feathered edge baked into the image. Do not ship a hard-edged portrait floating on a flat field.

### 7.4 Copy

**Approved (launch)**

> # TRADITION, REIMAGINED
> Handwoven silk and zari, in colours made for today.
> **Explore the collection →**

*Approved exception:* the hero headline is set in uppercase (Inter Tight 400, tracking −0.015em, on two lines so it stays in the floor shadow). Uppercase stays reserved for eyebrows everywhere else. *The new Banarasi.* moves to the alternates.

**Alternates**, for rotation with future campaigns:

| Headline | Supporting line |
|---|---|
| The new Banarasi. | Handwoven silk and zari, in colours made for now. |
| Colour, woven. | Banarasi silk in magenta, marigold, chartreuse and more. |
| Woven for now. | The Banarasi, cut loose from the occasion. |
| Banarasi, in the present tense. | New sarees, handwoven in Varanasi. |

**Rules:** headline ≤ 5 words, sentence case, ends in a full stop. Supporting line ≤ 10 words. One CTA, text + arrow, never a filled button over the image.

### 7.5 Type on the hero

- Headline: `--font-size-h1` (not Display). The image is the display element, and the headline supports it.
- Colour: `--color-ivory` on the image.
- Legibility: a bottom gradient scrim, `linear-gradient(to top, rgba(30,22,16,0.35) 0%, transparent 35%)`. It should never be noticeable as a gradient.

Fixed structure. No additions.

### 01 — Hero
See §7.

### 02 — New Arrivals

**Purpose:** show the newest sarees as fashion pieces.

**Desktop layout**
- Section header on one line: `New arrivals` (H2) left-aligned; `View all →` right-aligned on the same baseline.
- Three products, each spanning 4 of 12 columns, **full-body vertical 3:4** model images (C, D, E).
- The **centre column is offset down by 96px** (`--space-10`). It's a small break that turns a grid into a composition. Pure CSS, no custom interaction.
- Beyond three products: the row becomes a horizontal scroll with 3.3 cards visible, so the next card peeks in. No arrows; a thin 1px progress line below the row.

**Product information** (below the image, left-aligned, 16px below):
```
Kairi                         ← product title
Chartreuse and rose ombré, silk  ← descriptor, muted
₹ XX,XXX                      ← price
```
- No badges, hearts, "NEW" labels, ratings, quick-add buttons, colour swatches or borders.
- The only permitted overlay: `Sold out` as a caption-size label, top-left of the image, on an ivory chip.
- Hover (desktop): image scales to 1.03 over 1200ms; if a detail image exists it crossfades in over 600ms.

### 04 — Colour Story

**Purpose:** make colour, the brand's signature, an editorial moment instead of a filter UI.

**Concept: "Colour, woven."** A row of tall, flat colour fields, each sampled from a real saree, each named and paired with a textile macro of the saree it came from.

**Desktop layout**
- Headline block, left, columns 1–4: eyebrow `Colour story` · H2 `Colour, woven.` (with *woven* set in the editorial serif italic) · one line of body copy · `Shop all sarees →`.
- Columns 5–12: **five vertical colour fields**, 3:5 ratio, 8px gaps, flush together as a spectrum.
- Each field: flat content colour + colour name bottom-left in ivory (or charcoal on Chartreuse) at caption size.
- **Hover:** the field slowly crossfades (800ms) to the textile macro of that colour. The name stays.
- Fields: **Rani** (magenta) · **Marigold** · **Chartreuse** · **Wine** · **Indigo**.

**Mobile:** fields become a horizontal snap-scroll at 70vw. Each card is split, colour field on top and textile macro below, because touch has no hover.

**Why this works:** it's the only place on the site where jewel tones become UI surfaces. Keeping them contained to this section makes them feel intentional and makes the section feel like a spread in a magazine.

**Not:** a colour-filter widget, colour-dot swatches, a gradient, animated colour cycling.

### 05 — The Art of Banarasi

**Purpose:** establish craft authority in one calm, confident beat.

**Desktop layout**
- Background: `--color-surface` (stone). It's the only section with a background shift before the footer, marking a change of pace.
- Image: textile macro (interim: D, the rose-zari border meeting the wine body with gold buti), columns 1–6, 4:5, allowed to **bleed off the left edge** of the page (an editorial break from the grid).
- Text: columns 8–11, vertically centred against the image:

> *eyebrow:* The craft
> ## The Art of Banarasi
> Banarasi weaving is defined by its silk, its zari and its motifs — each one set on the loom, thread by thread, by weavers in Varanasi. It is slow, exacting work. It is also why no two of our sarees are quite alike.
> **Discover the craft →**

- Copy ≤ 60 words. No timeline, no dates, no statistics, no map, no loom illustration.

### 06 — Festive Edit

**Purpose:** the second campaign moment, emotional and full-scale.

**Desktop layout (horizontal campaign)**
- Background: `--color-ink` (the only dark section on the page, used once).
- Columns 1–7: image B full-body vertical, running full section height (min 90vh).
- Columns 9–12, top: a textile detail crop from B (the marigold border at the hem), 1:1, small.
- Columns 9–12, bottom-aligned with the main image:

> *eyebrow:* The festive edit
> ## For evenings, celebrations and everything worth dressing for.
> **Shop the edit →**

- All text in ivory. No sparkles, diyas, lights, or "Diwali Sale" styling. The festive feeling comes entirely from the image.

**Seasonal swap:** this section is a reusable campaign banner. Out of festive season, the same section runs the next campaign story.

See §12.8.

## 8b. Product Detail Page

The PDP is a fashion editorial that happens to be shoppable. Section order: Hero (gallery + sticky info) → Saree Swatch (a textile specimen: an extreme crop of the saree's own photo over a band in its colour) → On the body (the fall, the pallu, the border) → Colour chapter → The craft → About the saree → Style it → From the edit / Complete the story.

**Approved exceptions, for the PDP only**

| Rule elsewhere | PDP exception |
|---|---|
| Content colours appear only in Colour Story (§9.4) | **One** colour chapter per PDP: a pinned, scroll-driven sequence (the colour → the drape → the weave) in which the saree's colour spreads from the centre of the screen, shifts within its own tonal family (base → lighter → deeper), then contracts back to ivory. Text switches between ink and ivory per tone and never drops below 4.5:1. The colour also appears as the Saree Swatch band and the plane behind "The fall". No section repeats an image or title the chapter shows. |
| Don't reuse the disc as a UI shape (§5.1) | **At most two** geometric shapes per PDP: the plane behind "The fall" (rectangle) and the chapter disc, which drifts slowly while the photo's frame morphs from rectangle to column to circle. Both use the product's colours, never decoration. |
| Uppercase only for eyebrows (§10.3) | The giant colour name and the Saree Swatch colour name are uppercase. |

**Data rule:** every row, caption and section renders only from real product data. Missing data hides the row or section; it is never filled with placeholder copy. Style it stays hidden until styling photography exists.

**Motion rules for the chapter:** scroll position drives everything; nothing snaps, intercepts the wheel or waits for an animation. Under reduced motion, states switch instantly with no zoom, drift or spread. Parallax stays off elsewhere (§13).

## 9. Colour System

### 9.1 Principle

Three layers, strictly separated:

1. **Foundation colours:** the interface. Warm neutrals pulled from the studio walls. Almost always on screen.
2. **Content colours:** the jewel tones sampled from the sarees. They appear *only* in photography and in the Colour Story fields. They are never buttons, links, borders or icons.
3. **Functional colours:** error and success. Rare.

### 9.2 Foundation palette

| Token | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-bg` | Ivory | `#F6F1EA` | Primary page background. Warm, not cream, not yellow. |
| `--color-surface` | Stone | `#EDE5DA` | Secondary background: craft section, drawers, input fills, image placeholders |
| `--color-surface-2` | Sand | `#E2D7C8` | Hover on surface, skeleton loaders |
| `--color-text` | Ink | `#1F1A16` | Primary text, filled buttons, icons |
| `--color-text-secondary` | Umber | `#4A413A` | Secondary text, body copy on long reads |
| `--color-text-muted` | Taupe Grey | `#6E645B` | Descriptors, captions, metadata (5.3:1 on Ivory, AA) |
| `--color-border` | Linen | `#DDD3C6` | Hairlines, input borders, dividers |
| `--color-border-strong` | Clay Line | `#B9AB9A` | Focus-adjacent, active input borders. Never used for text. |
| `--color-ivory` | Ivory | `#F6F1EA` | Text on images and on Ink |
| `--color-ink` | Ink | `#1F1A16` | The single dark section (Festive), footer bottom bar |

**Interactive**

| Token | Hex | Use |

## 10. Typography

### 10.1 Typefaces

| Role | Family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary: display & UI** | **Inter Tight** (OFL) | A neutral, contemporary grotesk with tight default spacing. It looks editorial at large sizes and crisp in UI. |
| **Body** | **Inter** (OFL) | Same design DNA as Inter Tight, optimised for reading at small sizes |
| **Editorial accent** | **Instrument Serif**, *italic only* (OFL) | A narrow, contemporary serif for occasional contrast, e.g. one word in a headline. Deliberately not a "luxury" serif. |

**Upgrade path:** when budget allows, license a characterful grotesk (a Suisse- or Neue Haas-style family) to replace Inter Tight for display. Nothing else in the system changes.

**Loading:** self-host the WOFF2 files in the theme `assets/` folder with `@font-face` and `font-display: swap`. Subset to Latin + ₹. Preload only Inter Tight 400 and Inter 400.

**Weights in use:** 400 and 500 only. No bold (700) anywhere, and no light (300) below 40px.

### 10.2 Type scale

Fluid sizes via `clamp()`: mobile → desktop.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** | Inter Tight | 400 | 48 → 104px | 0.95 | −0.035em | Sentence |

| **H2** | Inter Tight | 400 | 28 → 40px | 1.08 | −0.02em | Sentence |

| **Body large** | Inter | 400 | 17 → 19px | 1.55 | −0.005em | Sentence |
| **Body** | Inter | 400 | 15 → 16px | 1.6 | 0 | Sentence |
| **Caption** | Inter | 400 | 12 → 13px | 1.45 | 0.005em | Sentence |

### 10.3 Rules

- **Uppercase is reserved for eyebrows** (and the wordmark). Headlines, nav, buttons and product titles are sentence or title case.
- **The serif italic appears at most once per section**, and only in H1/H2/Display, for one or two words: *Colour, woven.* Never for body copy, UI, or whole headlines.
- **Measure:** body copy 45–65 characters per line (`max-width: 34em`).
- **Hierarchy through size and space, not weight.** Headlines are 400; only H3, UI and titles use 500.
- **Prices:** `₹ 24,500` with a thin space after ₹, Indian digit grouping, no decimals. Compare-at prices only during genuine sale events, shown in muted with a strikethrough, never red.
- **Headlines end with a full stop** when they're statements (*The new Banarasi.*). Section labels don't (*New arrivals*).

- Beyond 1440px, content centres; **full-bleed images keep going** to the viewport edge.
- Container: `max-width: calc(1440px + 2 × margin)`.

### 11.2 Breaking the grid, on purpose

The grid is strict so that the breaks are noticeable. Permitted breaks:

1. **Full-bleed** (Hero, Festive): image to viewport edges.
2. **Edge bleed** (Art of Banarasi): image runs off one side of the page, text stays in the grid.
3. **Vertical offset** (New Arrivals centre card, Edit stack): offsets by `--space-10` (96px) or `--space-11` (128px) only.
4. **Empty column** (Shop by Edit): column 8 left intentionally empty.

Not permitted: rotated elements, overlapping text-on-image collages, masked shapes, diagonal sections, random offsets.

## 12. Components

Each component maps to a Shopify section or snippet (see §17).

### 12.1 Buttons & links

| Type | Style | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Text link + arrow** (default) | Button/link type, Ink, `→` after with 6px gap. Hover: 1px underline animates in from left (400ms), arrow nudges 4px right. | Most CTAs: hero, sections, edits |
| **Underlined text** | Always-visible 1px underline, 3px offset | Inline links, footer, secondary actions |
| **Filled** | Ink fill, Ivory text, 48px height, 24px horizontal padding, `--radius-sm` (2px). Hover: `--color-accent-hover`. | **Add to bag, Checkout, newsletter submit.** Commerce actions only. |
| **Outline** | 1px Ink border, transparent fill, same size as filled | Secondary commerce action (e.g. "Size & care guide" on PDP) |

**Never:** pill shapes, gradients, gold, shadows, icon-only primary buttons, more than one filled button per view (outside checkout).

Minimum touch target: 44×44px, including padding on text links.

### 12.2 Product card

- **No container.** The image is the card.
- Image: 3:4, `--color-surface` placeholder, lazy-loaded with `srcset`.
- Text block: 16px below image. Title → descriptor (4px gap) → price (8px gap).
- Hover: image scale 1.03 (1200ms), optional crossfade to detail image (600ms).
- Permitted overlay: `Sold out` only. No `New`, `Bestseller`, `% off`, hearts, quick-view.
- Whole card is one link; the wishlist (if ever added) is text on the PDP, not an icon on the card.

### 12.3 Editorial collection card

- Image at the ratio set by the layout (4:5 large, 5:4 small).
- Title (H3) · descriptor (Body, muted) · `Shop the edit →`.
- Text below the image, never overlaid.
- No border, no background, no shadow.

### 12.4 Header

- Height: 72px desktop, 56px mobile.
- **Layout:** primary nav left · wordmark centre · utilities right.
- **Over hero:** transparent; Ivory text and wordmark.
- **On scroll** (after 80% of hero height): Ivory background, Ink text, 1px `--color-border` bottom hairline, transition 300ms. Hides on scroll-down, reappears on scroll-up.
- **On all other pages:** solid Ivory from the start.

### 12.5 Navigation

**Primary (desktop)**
`Shop` · `Edits` · `Craft` · `About`

**Utilities (desktop)**, as text rather than icons, which reads as fashion rather than marketplace:
`Search` · `Account` · `Bag (2)`

**Shop menu:** a full-width drop panel on Ivory, three text columns plus one image.

**Mobile:** `☰` left · wordmark centre · search and bag icons right (1.5px line icons, 20px). The menu opens as a full-screen Ivory panel with large H2-size links, then a small secondary list (Account, Help, Instagram).

## 13. Interaction & Motion

**Character:** slow, soft, confident. Motion should feel like fabric settling, not like an app.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--duration-fast` | 200ms | Colour and opacity changes on links and buttons |
| `--duration-base` | 400ms | Underline draw, arrow nudge, header state |
| `--duration-slow` | 800ms | Section reveal, image crossfade, colour-field swap |
| `--duration-slower` | 1200ms | Image hover zoom |
| `--ease-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | Default for everything |
| `--ease-in-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` | Drawers, menus |

**Permitted**
- Image hover zoom: `scale(1.03)`, 1200ms.
- Reveal on scroll: opacity 0→1 and `translateY(16px)`→0, 800ms, **once per element**, staggered 80ms within a row. Images and text only.
- Hero: image fades in from 0 → 1 over 1200ms on load (after LCP), headline 200ms later.
- Colour Story fields: crossfade to textile macro on hover.
- Header background transition on scroll.
- Drawers (cart, menu): slide 400ms.

**Not permitted:** parallax, auto-advancing carousels, marquees, text-splitting letter animations, cursor followers, motif animations, confetti, sparkles, bounce/elastic easing, scroll-jacking.

**Reduced motion:** respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Disable reveal, zoom and hero fade; keep instant state changes.

**Feedback (under 400ms):** add-to-bag updates the bag count immediately (optimistic), the button shows `Added` for 2s, and the cart drawer opens. Every async action has a visible pending state.

## 15. Brand Voice

### 15.1 Character

**Confident · short · cultured · contemporary · warm · understated.**

She talks like someone with good taste and nothing to prove: a friend who works in fashion, not a salesperson and not a historian.

### 15.2 Principles

1. **Say less.** Cut the second sentence. Then check whether the first can lose a clause.
2. **Be specific.** Name the colour, the motif, the border. "Marigold border" beats "vibrant hues".
3. **Present tense, present day.** The craft is old. The brand isn't.
4. **Warm, not gushing.** One adjective at a time.
5. **Heritage is a fact, not a sales pitch.** State it plainly and move on.
6. **Hindi words only where English has no better word:** saree, pallu, zari, buti, jaal. Never decoratively.

### 15.3 Tone references

*Banarasi, reimagined.* · *Woven in colour.* · *Made for now.* · *Silk, zari, and a little more colour.* · *Craft with a contemporary point of view.*

These set the register. They are not taglines to repeat.

### 15.4 Banned phrases

"Timeless elegance" · "Where tradition meets modernity" · "Embrace your heritage" · "Celebrating the rich heritage of India" · "Luxury sarees for the modern woman" · "Exquisite" · "Regal" · "Fit for a queen" · "Drape yourself in…" · "Grace and poise" · "Ethnic wear" · "Shop now!!" · "Hurry, limited stock!"

### 15.5 Product naming

One word, occasionally two, taken from Indian everyday life (fruit, flowers, seasons, places), chosen for colour and mood. Easy to say, easy to remember.

| Saree | Name | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| Chartreuse–rose ombré (C) | **Kairi** (raw mango) | Chartreuse and rose ombré, silk |
| Wine with gold buti (D) | **Jamun** | Wine with rose zari border, silk |
| Indigo with lotus buti (E) | **Neel** | Indigo with aqua-silver border, silk |
| Magenta tree-of-life (A/B) | **Bagh** (garden) | Magenta jaal with marigold border, silk |

*Confirm fibre/weave (katan, kadhua, tissue, etc.) for each SKU and use it in the descriptor.*

### 15.6 Microcopy

| Context | Write | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Add to bag | Add to bag | ADD TO CART NOW |
| Added | Added | Item successfully added to your cart! |
| Empty bag | Your bag is empty. Start with the new arrivals → | Oops! Nothing here yet 😢 |
| Sold out | Sold out · Notify me | OUT OF STOCK |
| Newsletter success | Thank you — you're on the list. | Congratulations! You have successfully subscribed! |
| Error | That email doesn't look right. Check it and try again. | Invalid input. |
| 404 | This page has unravelled. Back to the collection → | 404 ERROR PAGE NOT FOUND |

### 15.7 Product description template

> **[Name]** is [what it is, in one line: the body colour, the motif, the border].
> [One line on how it wears or where it goes.]
>
> Silk · [weave] · Handwoven in Varanasi · 5.5m with unstitched blouse piece · Dry clean

Example:

> Made for a daytime celebration, or anything that needs more colour.

## 17. Shopify Implementation Principles

### 17.1 Theme foundation

- Build on **Dawn** (Shopify's free Online Store 2.0 reference theme) or an equally lean OS 2.0 theme. Strip its default styling back to this system rather than layering on top of it.
- All tokens live in one `assets/base-tokens.css` file (see §18), loaded before everything else.
- Map the key tokens to **theme settings** (`config/settings_schema.json`) so colours and fonts are editable, but ship with these values as defaults and treat changes as a brand decision, not a merchandising one.
- No page builders or apps for layout. Sections do the work.

### 17.2 Section map

| Section file | Homepage use | Key settings |
|---|---|---|
| `announcement-bar.liquid` | Optional | Text, link, enable toggle |
| `header.liquid` | Global | Menu, wordmark SVG, transparent-over-first-section toggle |
| `hero-campaign.liquid` | 01 | Desktop image, mobile image (separate pickers), headline, supporting line, CTA label + link, text position (bottom-left / bottom-centre), scrim strength (0–40%) |
| `product-row-editorial.liquid` | 02 | Collection, product limit, heading, "View all" link, offset-centre toggle, card image source (campaign metafield / featured image) |
| `edits-asymmetric.liquid` | 03 | Blocks: edit (collection, image override, title, descriptor, CTA). Layout: large-left / large-right. |
| `colour-story.liquid` | 04 | Heading (supports `<em>` for serif accent), body, CTA; blocks: colour (name, hex, macro image) × 3–6 |
| `image-with-text-editorial.liquid` | 05 | Image, image side, bleed-to-edge toggle, background (Ivory / Stone), eyebrow, heading, body, CTA |
| `campaign-feature.liquid` | 06 | Main image, detail image, theme (Ink / Ivory), eyebrow, heading, CTA |
| `footer.liquid` | Global | Menus ×3, social links, newsletter heading + copy |

Each section is reusable on collection landing pages, the Craft page and campaign pages. The homepage looks custom because of **composition and content**, not because of one-off code.

### 17.3 Data model: metafields

| Owner | Metafield | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | `custom.descriptor` | Single-line text | "Chartreuse and rose ombré, silk" under the title |
| Product | `custom.campaign_image` | File (image) | Model/campaign image used on cards in editorial sections |
| Product | `custom.detail_image` | File (image) | Textile detail for hover crossfade |
| Product | `custom.colour_family` | List of metaobject refs | Links to Colour Story entries; powers collection filtering later |
| Product | `custom.weave`, `custom.care` | Text | PDP specification block |
| Collection | `custom.edit_descriptor` | Single-line text | One-line edit description |
| Collection | `custom.edit_image` | File (image) | Editorial image for the edit card |
| Metaobject | `colour` (name, hex, macro image) | — | Single source for Colour Story and future filters |

**Card image logic:** `campaign_image` if present → otherwise `featured_image`. Editorial sections use campaign images, and collection grids can fall back to flat product shots without breaking the design.

### 17.4 Technical guardrails

- **Performance:** LCP hero ≤ 2.5s on 4G. Preload the hero image and two font files. Everything below the fold is lazy. No carousel libraries, no animation libraries: use CSS transitions plus one small IntersectionObserver for reveals.
- **Image quality:** upload masters at 3000px on the long edge minimum. Shopify's CDN serves WebP/AVIF automatically.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA. Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (Taupe Grey on Ivory passes; Clay Line does not and is never text). Visible focus rings. Menus operable by keyboard. Meaningful `alt` text describing the saree ("Magenta Banarasi silk saree with marigold border, worn with a sleeveless brocade blouse").
- **Apps:** only what commerce needs (reviews on PDP later, if ever; shipping; analytics). No app may inject UI onto the homepage without review against this document.
- **WhatsApp/chat:** if required, a small Ink line icon in the bottom-right, 44px, no green bubble, no auto-open.
